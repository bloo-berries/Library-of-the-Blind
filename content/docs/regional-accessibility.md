Accessibility technologies, standards, and infrastructure organized by geographic region.

## North America

### Languages
- English, Spanish, French (Canada)

### Infrastructure
- **Screen Readers:** Apple VoiceOver, JAWS, NVDA, Narrator
- **Braille Standards:** Unified English Braille (UEB)
- **Organizations:**
  - American Printing House for the Blind (APH)
  - National Federation of the Blind (NFB)
  - Canadian National Institute for the Blind (CNIB)

## Latin America

### Languages
- Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil)

### Infrastructure
- **Braille Translation:** Liblouis-based translators
- **Organizations:** "Biblioteca del Ciego" networks
- **Standards:** Spanish Braille, Portuguese Braille (PT-BR)

## Europe

### Languages
- English, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Russian, Polish, Dutch, and many others

### Infrastructure
- **Standards:** European Union's "Accessible Europe" guidelines
- **Organizations:** National Braille institutes in many countries
- **Braille Systems:** Country-specific codes
- **Technology:** Strong infrastructure, EU-wide accessibility initiatives

## Africa

### Languages
- Arabic, French, English, Swahili, Hausa, Amharic, Yoruba, Zulu, Xhosa, and many others

### Infrastructure
- **Organizations:** African Braille Consortium
- **Services:** Regional audio-book services
- **Braille Systems:** Many based on French or English Braille
- **Challenges:** Limited resources in many regions

## Middle East & North Africa

### Languages
- Arabic, Persian (Farsi), Hebrew, Kurdish, Turkish

### Infrastructure
- **Braille Standards:** Arabic Braille, Hebrew Braille, Persian Braille
- **Technology:** Strong RTL support for Arabic and Hebrew
- **Screen Readers:** Localized with right-to-left support

## Asia

### Languages
- Mandarin Chinese, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, and many others

### Infrastructure
- **Braille Systems:**
  - Chinese Braille (two-cell system)
  - Japanese Tenji
  - Korean Hangul Braille
  - Indian Braille (Bharati Braille - unified across 22 languages)
- **Technology:** Strong infrastructure in major languages

## Oceania

### Languages
- English, Maori, Samoan, Tok Pisin

### Infrastructure
- **Organizations:** New Zealand Braille Authority, Australian Braille Authority
- **Standards:** Unified English Braille (UEB)
- **Technology:** Strong English support, growing indigenous language support

## Regional Patterns

### Language Coverage
- **High Coverage:** English, French, German, Spanish, Arabic, Japanese, Chinese
- **Medium Coverage:** Many European languages, major Asian languages
- **Growing Coverage:** Indigenous languages, minority languages

### Infrastructure Development
- **Mature:** North America, Europe, parts of Asia
- **Developing:** Latin America, Africa, parts of Asia
- **Emerging:** Many regions with minority languages

## Notes

- Infrastructure varies significantly by region
- Major languages typically have better support
- Community-driven efforts are crucial for minority languages
- International collaboration is increasing
