Physical and tactile technologies that provide access to information through touch.

## Refreshable Braille Displays

Electronic devices that dynamically display Braille characters using pins that raise and lower.

### Major Manufacturers
- **[Freedom Scientific](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_Scientific)** - Focus 40, Focus 80
- **[HumanWare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BrailleNote)** - BrailleNote Touch, BrailleSense
- **[APH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Printing_House_for_the_Blind)** - Various models
- **[Orbit Research](https://www.orbitresearch.com/)** - Orbit Reader

### Language Support
Full support for English, French, German, Spanish, Arabic (RTL), Japanese, Chinese, and many more.

## Talking-Touch Devices

Portable computing devices that combine Braille display and speech output.

- **BrailleSense** - Portable device with Braille display and speech
- **BrailleNote Touch** - Integrated TTS + refreshable display
- **Mantis Q40** - QWERTY + Braille display
- **Chameleon 20** - Ultra-portable display

## 3D Printed Models

Three-dimensional tactile representations for education and understanding.

### Applications
- **Anatomical models** - Medical education
- **Architectural models** - Building layouts
- **Scientific models** - Molecules, cells, geological formations
- **Mathematical models** - Geometric shapes, graphs
- **Art reproductions** - Tactile versions of sculptures and paintings

## Tactile Graphics

Raised-line diagrams, maps, and illustrations that can be felt.

### Types
- **Embossed diagrams** - Created using embossing machines
- **Swell paper graphics** - Heat-raised graphics
- **Vacuum-formed graphics** - 3D-like raised surfaces
- **Graphic Braille** - Combination of text and graphics

### Standards
- **ISO 13125** - Tactile map symbols (standardized symbols for roads, water, elevation)

## Braille Production Equipment

- **Perkins Brailler** - Mechanical Brailler
- **Mountbatten Brailler** - Electronic classroom Brailler
- **Smart Brailler** - Electronic with audio feedback
- **Slate and Stylus** - Manual Braille writing
- **ViewPlus Embossers** - Tiger embossing technology
- **Index Embossers** - Swedish embossing systems

## Navigation and Mobility Aids

- **BlindSquare** - GPS/POI navigation app
- **Seeing AI** (Microsoft) - Image recognition
- **Be My Eyes** - Volunteer visual assistance
- **Aira** - Live visual interpreter service
- **WeWALK** - Smart cane with haptic feedback
- **UltraCane** - Ultrasonic obstacle detection

## Experimental Systems

### Vibrotactile Alphabets
- Patterns of vibration on skin representing letters
- Used in wearable devices
- Research ongoing since 1960s

### Optacon Letter Recognition (Historical)
- Camera converts print to vibrating pin array
- Felt by fingertip
- USA, 1970s-1990s (discontinued)

### Electrotactile Displays
- Electrical stimulation patterns representing text
- Research/experimental since 1980s

### Other Technologies
- **Haptic Belt navigation** - Vibrating belt directional cues
- **Bone conduction + haptics** - Combined modalities
- **Electrotactile tongue displays** - BrainPort technology

## Notes

- Refreshable Braille displays are essential for digital access
- 3D models make abstract concepts tangible
- Tactile graphics provide access to visual information
- Standards like ISO 13125 ensure consistency
