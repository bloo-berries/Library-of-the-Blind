## Major Asian Braille Codes

### [Arabic Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Arabic, Persian (Farsi), Urdu (Pakistan)
- **Key Characteristics:** Modern Arabic Braille uses the French sorting order and is adapted for right-to-left reading conventions.
- **Status:** Active

### [Pakistani Urdu Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Urdu_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Urdu (Pakistan)
- **Key Characteristics:** Pakistan has not adopted Bharati braille, so the Urdu Braille of Pakistan is an entirely different alphabet than the Urdu Braille of India
- **Status:** Active

### [Japanese Tenji (Braille)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_Braille)
- **Inventor/Developer:** Kuraji Ishikawa (developed in 1890)
- **Languages Supported:** Japanese kana (hiragana/katakana) and kanji (via "kanji Braille")
- **Key Characteristics:** Based on the original braille script. It transcribes Japanese as it would be written in hiragana or katakana syllabaries, without any provision for writing kanji.
- **Status:** Active

### [Korean Hangul Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Braille)
- **Inventor/Developer:** Rosetta Sherwood Hall (1894), Park Du-seong (1926)
- **Languages Supported:** Korean Hangul
- **Key Characteristics:** Reflects the patterns found in Hangul and differentiates initial consonants, vowels, and final consonants. 24-cell base, combines jamo
- **Status:** Active

### [Mainland Chinese Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Standard Chinese (Mandarin)
- **Key Characteristics:** Each syllable is written with up to three Braille cells, representing the initial, final, and tone
- **Status:** Active

### [Two-Cell Chinese Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two-cell_Chinese_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Chinese (Mandarin)
- **Key Characteristics:** Encodes all syllable information (onset, rime, tone) in exactly two cells
- **Status:** Active

### [Cantonese Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantonese_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Cantonese (Hong Kong and Macau)
- **Key Characteristics:** Purely phonetic. Each syllable is divided into three parts: the initial consonant, the rime, and the tone
- **Status:** Active

### [Taiwanese Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Taiwanese Mandarin
- **Key Characteristics:** Based on the Zhuyin (Bopomofo) phonetic system rather than Pinyin
- **Status:** Active

### [Indian Braille (Bharati Braille)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bharati_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Assamese, Oriya, Kannada, Sanskrit, Marathi, Nepali, Urdu (India), Sinhala, and others
- **Key Characteristics:** Unified code across 22+ Indian languages. Adopted by Sri Lanka, Nepal, and Bangladesh.
- **Status:** Active
- **Regional Variants:** Hindi/Devanagari, Tamil, Bengali, Kannada, Telugu, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi/Gurmukhi, Odia, Sinhala, Nepali

### [Thai Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Thai
- **Key Characteristics:** Adapted for Thai abugida with tone markers
- **Status:** Active

### [Vietnamese Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Vietnamese
- **Key Characteristics:** Phonetically based on Quoc ngu romanization. Uses diacritic cells for tonal marks
- **Status:** Active

### [Malay/Indonesian Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Malay, Indonesian
- **Key Characteristics:** Based on Latin alphabet. Derived from English Braille with local letters
- **Status:** Active

### [Mongolian Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongolian_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Mongolian (Cyrillic script)
- **Key Characteristics:** Based on Russian Braille
- **Status:** Active

### [Turkish Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Turkish
- **Key Characteristics:** Adapted for Turkish alphabet
- **Status:** Active

### [Georgian Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Georgian
- **Key Characteristics:** Unique mapping
- **Status:** Active

### [Armenian Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenian_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Armenian
- **Key Characteristics:** Distinct cell assignments
- **Status:** Active

### [Tibetan Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tibetan_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Tibetan
- **Key Characteristics:** Represents Tibetan syllabic structure; developed mid-20th century
- **Status:** Active

### [Burmese Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burmese_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Burmese (Myanmar)
- **Key Characteristics:** For Burmese script
- **Status:** Active

### [Khmer Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Khmer (Cambodia)
- **Key Characteristics:** For Cambodian script
- **Status:** Active

### [Lao Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lao_Braille)
- **Languages Supported:** Lao
- **Key Characteristics:** Uses Japanese vowels; adapted for Lao script
- **Status:** Active
