Additional resources, references, and links related to blind and visually impaired communication methodologies.

## Organizations

### International
- **[World Blind Union (WBU)](https://www.worldblindunion.org/)** - Global organization representing blind persons
- **[International Council on English Braille (ICEB)](https://iceb.org/)** - English Braille standards
- **[DeafBlind International](https://www.deafblindinternational.org/)** - Global DeafBlind advocacy
- **[Helen Keller International](https://www.hki.org/)** - Global health and vision
- **[Sightsavers](https://www.sightsavers.org/)** - Global eye health organization

### Regional
- **[American Printing House for the Blind (APH)](https://www.aph.org/)** - USA
- **[Royal National Institute of Blind People (RNIB)](https://www.rnib.org.uk/)** - UK
- **[Canadian National Institute for the Blind (CNIB)](https://www.cnib.ca/)** - Canada
- **[Perkins School for the Blind](https://www.perkins.org/)** - USA (historical significance)
- **[National Federation of the Blind (NFB)](https://www.nfb.org/)** - USA advocacy
- **[ONCE](https://www.once.es/)** - Spain (lottery-funded model)
- **[Vision Australia](https://www.visionaustralia.org/)** - Australia

## Standards

### Braille Standards
- **[Unified English Braille (UEB)](https://www.iceb.org/ueb.html)** - International English standard
- **[Bharati Braille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bharati_Braille)** - Unified Indian standard
- **[ISO 13125](https://www.iso.org/standard/57164.html)** - Tactile map symbols
- **[BANA](https://www.brailleauthority.org/)** - North American standards
- **[UKAAF](https://www.ukaaf.org/)** - UK accessible format standards

### Accessibility Standards
- **[WCAG](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/quickref/)** - Web Content Accessibility Guidelines
- **[Section 508](https://www.section508.gov/)** - US accessibility law
- **[EN 301 549](https://www.etsi.org/deliver/etsi_en/301500_301599/301549/)** - EU ICT accessibility
- **[ISO 14289 (PDF/UA)](https://www.iso.org/standard/64599.html)** - Accessible PDF standard

### International Frameworks
- **[Marrakesh Treaty](https://www.wipo.int/treaties/en/ip/marrakesh/)** - Copyright exception for accessible formats
- **[UN CRPD](https://www.un.org/development/desa/disabilities/)** - Convention on Rights of Persons with Disabilities
