### Teaching Methodologies
- **[Mangold Program](https://www.secondsense.org/)** - Dr. Sally Mangold's developmental teaching method for tactile perception and Braille letter recognition, demonstrated in 1978 research. A foundational methodology for Braille instruction that established systematic approaches to developing tactile discrimination skills and Braille reading fluency. While pedagogical rather than a script, it is a foundational methodology in Braille education

## Historical Resources

### Historical Documents and Key Histories
- **[The "War of the Dots"](https://www.afb.org/about-afb/history/online-museum/timeline-afbs-history)** - Robert B. Irwin's documentation of the competition between American Braille, New York Point, and British Braille in the late 19th and early 20th centuries (see Irwin's *As I Saw It*)
- **[Pamela Lorimer's Thesis](https://www.duxburysystems.org/downloads/library/history/lorimer.htm)** - Comprehensive history of braille development (Duxbury history library)
- **[TSBVI "A Brief History of Tactile Writing Systems"](https://www.tsbvi.edu/wp-content/uploads/assets/documents/irl/brief_history_tactile_writing_systems.pdf)** - Overview of pre-Braille and Braille writing systems
- **[UNESCO World Braille Usage (1953)](https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000013894)** - First comprehensive international survey of braille codes
- **[World Braille Usage, 3rd edition (2013)](https://www.perkins.org/resource/world-braille-usage/)** - Current authoritative reference for braille codes worldwide
